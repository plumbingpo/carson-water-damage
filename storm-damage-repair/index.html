How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Know how to shut off your main water supply in case a storm damages a pipe
  • Install impact-resistant windows or storm shutters if you live in a hurricane-prone area
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Cracked caulk lets blown rain in, so inspect and renew the seals on doors and windows
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Water pooling in the attic, around the chimney or near skylights
  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Water stains that show up on ceilings or walls during or after a storm
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
